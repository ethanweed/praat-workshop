Challenges
1. Advanced
Using what we have learned today (Praat scripts, clear history, paste history), plus poking around in the Praat menus, create a script that makes a spectrogram of a Praat-synthesised voice saying a phrase of your choice. Hint: find the feature called "Text-to-speech synthesis". Save your script in your "scripts" folder.
2. Extra advanced! Record yourself saying something. Then make a script that:
  1. Reads in your recording and creates a spectrogram
  2. Makes a spectrogram of the text-to-speech synthesizer saying the same thing
  3. Plots your spectrogram at the top, and the TTS spectrogram below it
  4. Adds titles above each spectrogram
  5. Exports to a PDF or PNG in your "output" folder
  6. Saves your script to your "scripts" folder

Homework: record your own vowels
In a quiet location, make a recording of yourself saying “heed, hid, head, had, hod, hawed, hood, who’d”, in English.
You have probably never had reason to say the word "hod" before, but try to say it so that it rhymes with “bod”, “cod”, or “sod"
Leave a little space between each word, to make it easier for the Praat script to seperate.
